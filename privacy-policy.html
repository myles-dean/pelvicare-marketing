PELVICARE PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: March 01, 2026

1. Overview

PelviCare LLC (“PelviCare,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and safeguard personal information when you access or use our website, mobile application, subscription services, and related features (collectively, the “Platform”).

PelviCare operates as a consumer wellness and educational platform. We do not provide medical services and do not create medical records.

For purposes of this Policy, “personal information” includes “consumer health data” and “sensitive personal information” where applicable under state law.

2. Information We Collect

PelviCare collects personal information reasonably necessary and proportionate to operate the Platform and provide subscription-based educational services. We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Billing information
  • Subscription status

B. Usage Data

  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Browser type
  • Interaction data
  • Log data

C. Quiz and Wellness Information

You may voluntarily provide responses to wellness quizzes, which may include information regarding:

  • Pelvic floor symptoms
  • Incontinence
  • Pelvic pain
  • Pregnancy or postpartum status
  • Menopausal status
  • Bone health concerns

This information is used solely to direct you to appropriate educational programming.

PelviCare does not use quiz responses for medical diagnosis or treatment purposes.

3. Algorithmic Processing

Quiz responses are processed algorithmically to recommend educational content pathways.

Quiz responses:

  • Are not reviewed by healthcare providers for clinical decision-making;
  • Do not create a medical record;
  • Are not used to provide individualized treatment;
  • Are not retained beyond the period reasonably necessary to generate programming recommendations and maintain system integrity, unless required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

PelviCare does not use automated processing to make decisions that produce legal, financial, medical, or similarly significant effects concerning users.

4. No HIPAA-Covered Activity

PelviCare is not a healthcare provider delivering medical services through the Platform and does not bill insurance.

Accordingly, PelviCare is not acting as a “covered entity” under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) in connection with Platform activities.

Information submitted through the Platform is not protected health information under HIPAA.

Users should not submit medical records, diagnostic reports, or protected health information through the Platform.

5. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide access to subscription services;
  • Process payments;
  • Recommend educational programming;
  • Improve Platform functionality;
  • Communicate with users; and
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud.

PelviCare does not sell personal information.

6. Disclosure of Information

We may disclose personal information to the following categories of service providers:

  • Payment processors;
  • Hosting providers;
  • Analytics providers; and
  • Customer service vendors.

PelviCare requires service providers to process personal information solely for the purpose of providing contracted services and prohibits further use or disclosure except as required by law.

PelviCare does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise monetize personal information.

7. Data Retention

PelviCare retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain business records.

Consumer health quiz responses are not retained beyond the period reasonably necessary to generate programming recommendations and maintain system integrity, unless required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backup system constraints.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

9. WASHINGTON CONSUMER HEALTH DATA ADDENDUM

(Compliance with Washington My Health My Data Act)

This Addendum applies solely to consumers residing in the State of Washington and is incorporated into the PelviCare Privacy Policy.
